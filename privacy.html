Privacy Policy — PINGZY ⚡

Published by AetherByte Studios • Effective Date: October 2026

1. Overview & Privacy First Commitment

PINGZY ("the App"), developed and published by AetherByte Studios (engineered by Harsh Raj), is built on a strict privacy-first architecture. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal or financial data on external servers.

2. 100% Local On-Device Storage

  • All group names, participant names, expense splits, dues, and UPI IDs entered into PINGZY are stored strictly locally on your device using browser/app localStorage.
  • No account registration, phone number, or cloud login is required to use the App.
  • Clearing your app data or uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored records from your device.

3. Zero Ads & Zero Analytics Tracking

PINGZY contains zero third-party advertisements and does not use invasive background tracking SDKs or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Actions (WhatsApp & Payment Links)

When you use the 1-Tap Share or Reminder feature, PINGZY simply formats text on your device and opens WhatsApp or your chosen messaging app via standard device intents. We do not read your contacts, messages, or bank transactions.
