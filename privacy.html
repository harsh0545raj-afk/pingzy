Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: October 2026 • Developer: AetherByte Studios (Harsh Raj)

1. Zero Cloud Collection (100% Local Storage)

Pingzy is engineered by AetherByte Studios as a privacy-first, offline-capable personal ledger and bill-splitting utility. We do not operate any external database servers to collect, store, sell, or track your personal data.

  • All khata entries, friend names, phone numbers, split calculations, and optional UPI IDs are stored strictly inside your own device's local browser storage (localStorage).
  • You have full control to edit, delete, or export your data as a backup file at any time directly from the app.

2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Photos & Media (Optional Poster Studio): When you select a friend's photo to create a custom Movie Meme Poster, the image is processed 100% locally on your phone using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos are never uploaded to any server.
  • Contacts Picker (Optional): If you use the Contacts button, the app uses the standard Android Contact Picker API only to autofill the selected friend's name and 10-digit number into your form. We never read or upload your address book.
  • Notifications (Optional): If enabled in Studio settings, Pingzy triggers local on-device reminders for your pending dues. You can turn notifications OFF at any time with a single tap.

3. Financial & UPI Disclaimer

Pingzy is a personal calculation and reminder utility only.

  • Pingzy does not provide loans, credit, banking services, or financial advice.
  • Pingzy does not process payments or access your bank accounts. Optional UPI QR codes and payment links generated by the app simply open standard UPI applications installed on the user's device.

4. Third-Party Sharing (WhatsApp / SMS)

Messages, posters, or links are only shared when you explicitly tap the WhatsApp, Share Poster, or SMS buttons, which open your device's native sharing interface under your control.
